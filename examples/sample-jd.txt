Senior Backend Engineer — Payments Platform

About the role
We're building the money-movement platform behind thousands of businesses. You'll design and scale the services that move billions of dollars a year.

What you'll do
- Design, build and operate high-throughput services in Go and Python
- Own the ledger and payments APIs (REST and gRPC) end to end
- Improve reliability, observability and performance of our distributed systems
- Mentor engineers and lead technical design reviews

Requirements
- 5+ years of backend engineering experience
- Strong experience with Go or Python
- Deep knowledge of PostgreSQL and Redis
- Experience with Kafka or other message queues and event-driven architecture
- Experience running services on Kubernetes and AWS
- CI/CD and infrastructure as code (Terraform)

Nice to have
- Payments, fintech or ledger experience
- Experience with Snowflake or dbt
- Contributions to open source
